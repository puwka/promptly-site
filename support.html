Common fixes

  • Refresh the tab after installing or updating Promptly.
  • Confirm you are online — AI requests need network access.
  • If image analysis fails, try again or switch model family (GPT / Claude).
  • Free daily limit: wait until the next UTC day, or upgrade to Pro.
  • Pro weekly tokens exhausted: wait for the next week, or manage billing in Settings.

Billing

Manage or cancel Promptly Pro from the extension: Settings → Manage subscription (Stripe Customer Portal).
